Yoonsol Jung

Undergraduate Student, School of Computing, KAIST

I am an undergraduate student in the School of Computing at KAIST. My research interests lie in the safety, interpretability, and trustworthiness of AI agents, with a focus on understanding and reliably controlling the behavior of LLM-based agents. In the long term, I aim to extend this line of work to Physical AI, where agents act in the real world and failures carry physical consequences.

I am currently an undergraduate research intern at the Algorithmic Intelligence Lab (ALIN) at KAIST, advised by Prof. Jinwoo Shin. Previously, I worked on AI reliability as a co-op intern at NC AI.
